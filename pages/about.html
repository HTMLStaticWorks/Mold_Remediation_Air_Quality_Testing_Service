Our Genesis

Founded in Science.
Driven by Health.

AeroPure Environmental was founded in 2011 by two former university environmental microbiologists who grew frustrated seeing mold treated as a cosmetic painting problem rather than an urgent airborne health hazard.

We brought clinical medical-grade containment, laser spore counters, and accredited third-party laboratory verification into residential homes and commercial buildings. Over a decade later, we have cleared over 14,800 properties with zero fungal recurrence.

Core Philosophy

The Three Pillars of AeroPure Integrity

We believe every family, tenant, and employee has an inherent right to inhale air free of toxic pathogens.

Independent Verification

We never verify our own remediation work in-house. All clearance air samples are shipped to accredited external laboratories to ensure complete conflict-of-interest transparency.

Zero Toxic Off-Gassing

Traditional remediation floods living spaces with harsh chlorine or synthetic phenols. We rely on botanical thymol formulations and cold dry-ice sublimation that leave zero chemical residue.

Lifelong Accountability

Our transferable lifetime warranty is backed by continuous customer support, annual air monitoring options, and client portal records accessible indefinitely.
