Microbial Toxicology

Why Airborne Mold Is a Biological Hazard

Prolonged exposure to fungal hyphae and volatile mycotoxins systematically compromises immune defense and deteriorates timber integrity.

Respiratory & Neurological Strain

Mycotoxins produced by Stachybotrys chartarum irritate bronchial pathways, inducing chronic asthma attacks, migraine clusters, and cognitive fatigue.

Sub-Surface Framing Rot

Fungal mycelium digests cellulose in structural studs, floor joists, and subflooring, triggering dry rot and causing thousands in compromised load-bearing timber.

HVAC Spore Distribution

A localized colony inside a basement or crawlspace will be pulled into the return plenum, cycling active spores into every bedroom and living space hourly.

Real Estate Transaction Halts

Lenders, Fannie Mae, and VA home loan underwriters immediately fail appraisals upon mold discovery, placing property closings into mandatory escrow freeze.
