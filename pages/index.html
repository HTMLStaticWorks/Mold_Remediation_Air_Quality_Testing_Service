Scientific Methodology

From Detection to Certified Purity

Our rigorous 4-stage environmental workflow guarantees zero cross-contamination and verifiable air safety.

01

Inspect & Thermal Map

A certified industrial hygienist performs non-destructive infrared moisture mapping and air particulate baseline sampling.

02

Lab Spore Analysis

Air cassettes and bio-swabs are analyzed by an independent ISO-17025 accredited laboratory to identify genus and density.

03

Contain & Remediate

Negative-air containment zones are erected under continuous HEPA filtration to safely extract spores without home distribution.

04

Clearance Verification

Post-remediation PRV air testing proves indoor spore levels match or beat pristine outdoor baseline air before containment drops.
