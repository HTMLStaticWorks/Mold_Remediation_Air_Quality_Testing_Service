Diagnostic Phase 1

Infrared Thermal Mold Inspection

Mold cannot colonize without sustained moisture. Rather than punching destructive holes into your drywall, our certified thermographers map cold thermal anomalies that signify hidden pipe leaks, condensation buildup, and roof intrusions.

What's Included in Every Inspection:

FLIR Thermal Mapping

Detects temperature differentials created by concealed evaporation.

Sub-Surface Moisture Pinning

Measures exact wood and drywall moisture saturation percentage.

Cavity Borescope Video

Micro-endoscopic HD camera inspection inside wall voids.

Psychrometric Dew Point

HVAC relative humidity and condensation risk calculations.

Remediation Phase 2

Hospital-Grade Negative Air Remediation

Disturbing dry mold colonies causes billions of micro-spores to launch into the air. Our IICRC-certified crews construct sealed containment airlocks under continuous negative pressure before a single particle is touched.

Our Proprietary Eradication Protocol:

  • 6-Mil Polyethylene Airlocks: Physical containment with zippered decontaminating vestibules.
  • HEPA-500 Negative Air Scrubbers: 99.97% capture efficiency down to 0.3 microns vented outdoors.
  • Dry-Ice Timber Media Blasting: Non-abrasive frozen CO2 lifts fungal roots out of wood grain.
  • Botanical Bio-Shield Barrier: EPA-registered thymol sealant prevents dormant spore germination.

Diagnostic Phase 3

Laboratory Spore Trap & VOC Testing

Quantitative microbiological profiling to ensure your indoor breathing zone meets surgical-grade purity standards.

How We Test Your Air

We run calibrated 15-liter/minute vacuum pumps pulling ambient air through microscopic Zefon Air-O-Cell cassettes. We sample indoor suspect zones and simultaneous outdoor baseline air to determine whether spore concentrations exceed natural ambient ranges.

What Your Lab Certificate Discloses:

  • • Total Spores/m³ count by fungal genus (Stachybotrys, Aspergillus, Penicillium, Chaetomium)
  • • Total Volatile Organic Compounds (TVOC) in parts per billion (ppb)
  • • Laser particle counts for PM2.5 and PM10 respiratory irritants
  • • Official Post-Remediation Verification (PRV) Pass/Fail certificate signed by a CIH
