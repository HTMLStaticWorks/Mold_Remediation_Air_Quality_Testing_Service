Diagnostic Phase 3

Laboratory Spore Trap & VOC Testing

Quantitative microbiological profiling to ensure your indoor breathing zone meets surgical-grade purity standards.

How We Test Your Air

We run calibrated 15-liter/minute vacuum pumps pulling ambient air through microscopic Zefon Air-O-Cell cassettes. We sample indoor suspect zones and simultaneous outdoor baseline air to determine whether spore concentrations exceed natural ambient ranges.

What Your Lab Certificate Discloses:
