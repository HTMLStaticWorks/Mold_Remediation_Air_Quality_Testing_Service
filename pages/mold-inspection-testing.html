Diagnostic Checklist

Critical Signs You Need a Mold & Air Quality Test

If you notice any of these environmental red flags, invisible fungal micro-spores may already be circulating in your breathing zone.

Musty Smell

Recurring Musty Odors

Microbial volatile organic compounds (mVOCs) emitted by growing colonies create an earthy, basement smell when AC or heat runs.

Black Mold

Dark Staining & Discoloration

Dark green, black, or powdery white clusters around windows, bathroom baseboards, or basement framing that return after bleaching.

Allergies

Unexplained Allergy Symptoms

Chronic coughing, morning sneezing, itchy eyes, or asthma flares that improve when outside the property indicate spore exposure.

Water Leaks

Past Leaks or Flooding History

Any past plumbing burst, roof leak, or wet crawlspace that was not dried under industrial negative air within 24-48 hours.

Warped Walls

Peeling Paint & Drywall Warping

Bubbling plaster, sagging ceiling tiles, or warped floorboards indicate hidden moisture saturation where mold breeds rapidly.

Humidity

Indoor Humidity Over 55% RH

Window condensation sweating and damp basements crossing the 55% relative humidity dew point create optimal fungal reproduction.

Our 5-Stage Scientific Inspection & Testing Protocol

We leave nothing to guesswork. Every inspection follows standardized forensic microbiological methodology.

01

Psychrometric Baseline

We record indoor vs outdoor relative humidity, ambient temperature, and dew point to map condensation risk zones.

02

FLIR Thermal Scan

High-resolution infrared thermography pinpoints hidden moisture pockets and evaporative cooling behind drywall non-invasively.

03

Moisture Depth Pinning

Dual pin and pinless hydrometers measure exact %MC wood and drywall saturation depth to quantify structural rot risk.

04

Spore Trap Air Sampling

Calibrated 15 L/min vacuum pumps draw air into Zefon Air-O-Cell cassettes (target indoor zones + outdoor baseline).

05

AIHA Lab Analysis & Report

Independent accredited microbiology laboratory identifies genus, density, and issues an official CIH-signed certificate.
